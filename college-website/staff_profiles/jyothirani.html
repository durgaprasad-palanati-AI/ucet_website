Profile

Mrs. M. Jyothi Rani has been serving as Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at University College of Engineering & Technology, Mahatma Gandhi University, Nalgonda since 2 August 2016. She holds a B.Tech in Computer Science and Information Technology from Nagarjuna Institute of Technology and Sciences (JNTU Hyderabad) and an M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from Mannan Institute of Science and Technology (JNTU Hyderabad).

Prior to joining UCET, she served as Assistant Professor in the Department of CSE at NITS, and subsequently as Assistant Professor and Head of the Department of CSE at MIETW, where she handled both academic and administrative responsibilities and contributed to institutional technical events.

With 20 years of teaching experience, she is committed to continuous professional development. She has completed advanced Faculty Development Programmes (FDPs) sanctioned by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India, conducted by the E&ICT Academy, NIT Warangal. She has published three research papers in reputed International Journals.

At UCET, she has held key administrative roles including Head of the Department (CSE) and Assistant Coordinator, Examination Branch. She is currently serving as Admission Coordinator and is an active member of various institutional committees. She also serves as IQAC Coordinator at Mahatma Gandhi University and CSI Department Coordinator at UCET. She has extensive experience supervising undergraduate student projects and internships.
