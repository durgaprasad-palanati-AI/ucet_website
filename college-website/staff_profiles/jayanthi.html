Profile

Dr. M. Jayanthi is serving as Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science & Informatics at Mahatma Gandhi University, Nalgonda since 4th May 2012. She holds an M.Tech in CSE from JNTU Hyderabad, a Ph.D in CSE from Osmania University, Hyderabad, and is AP-SET Qualified. She brings 20 years of teaching experience to her role.

She has published 4 research papers in International Journals indexed in Scopus and has presented papers at IEEE and Springer Conferences. She organized 1 National Seminar as Convenor, reflecting her active engagement in academic outreach and knowledge dissemination.

Over her career she has held several notable administrative positions — Warden for Girls Hostel, Vice-Principal, Principal of UCET, Chairperson for the AICTE Approval Process Committee, In-charge Placement Officer, and Head of the Department of Computer Science and Informatics. She is presently serving as Co-ordinator for the Dean CDC, MGU, and has contributed to various College Level and University Level Committees.
