Profile

Dr. M. Ramachandru is currently serving as Assistant Professor (Contract) in the Department of Humanities and Science, University College of Engineering and Technology, Mahatma Gandhi University, Nalgonda.

He holds an M.Sc. in Mathematics from Osmania University (2012), a B.Ed. from Osmania University (2014), and was awarded his Ph.D. in Mathematics in 2024. He also qualified APSET in 2013 in Mathematical Sciences.

He has 11 years of teaching experience and 4 years of research experience, with active contributions in academic teaching at undergraduate and postgraduate levels.

He has published seven research papers in reputed journals and has presented papers in multiple national and international conferences. He has also participated in more than four Faculty Development Programmes.

In addition to teaching, he has served as In-charge Head of the Department and is currently NSS Programme Officer (Unit–III). He is also a member of admission and sports committees and has contributed to organizing national seminars and conferences.
