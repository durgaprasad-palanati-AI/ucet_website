Profile

Dr. Vijay Kumar Chityala is serving as Assistant Professor (Contract) in the Department of Basic Science and Humanities (Chemistry), University College of Engineering & Technology, Mahatma Gandhi University, Nalgonda, Telangana, since 11th November 2013.

He completed his M.Sc. in Chemistry from Osmania University in 2006 and qualified APSET in 2012. He was awarded his Ph.D. in Chemistry from Osmania University in 2015.

He has published 12 research papers in national and international journals and has actively participated in 19 conferences and seminars. He has received prestigious awards including Best Poster Award (NCRACR-2009) and Prof. G.K. Chaturvedi Award in Coordination Chemistry (ICC-2012).

He has also participated in refresher courses, workshops, faculty development programmes, and orientation programmes to enhance his academic and professional expertise.

In addition to teaching, he has served in several administrative roles including NSS Programme Officer, Head of Department (in-charge), member of various committees such as Anti-Ragging, Disciplinary, Sports, Cultural, Beautification, and Admissions committees, and Programme Convener for Induction Programmes.
