Profile

Dr. D. Sandhya Rani has been serving as Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science & Informatics at Mahatma Gandhi University, Nalgonda since 4th May 2012. She holds a B.Tech and M.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering from Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Hyderabad, and a Ph.D in CSE from Osmania University, Hyderabad, bringing with her a rich teaching experience spanning 17 years.

She has four research publications to her credit in National and International Journals indexed in Scopus, and has presented papers at National, International, and Springer Conferences. She also attended a three-day workshop on "Teaching, Learning & Evaluation online with Moodle MOOC Platform & Open Education Resources" at NUEPA, New Delhi.

Over the years, she has held several prestigious academic and administrative positions — including Warden for Girls Hostel (UCET, MGU), Head of the Department, Vice-Principal, and Principal of University College of Engineering and Technology, MGU. At present, she is serving as Assistant Controller of Examinations (PG Section), Examination Branch, MGU, and as Chairperson of the Board of Studies for Computer Science. She is an active member of various College Level and University Level Committees.
