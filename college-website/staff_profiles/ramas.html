Profile

Mr. R. Ramaswamy is serving as Assistant Professor (PT) in the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at University College of Engineering and Technology, Mahatma Gandhi University, Nalgonda. He joined the institution in 2023 and has 10 years of teaching experience in the field of Electrical and Electronics Engineering.

His teaching expertise includes Electrical Circuits, Electrical Machines, Power Systems, Signals and Systems, and Control Systems, providing strong foundational knowledge to undergraduate engineering students.

He has contributed to research in Robotics and Manufacturing Automation through one international conference publication. He has also actively participated in eight Faculty Development Programmes conducted online and completed one NPTEL certification course, enhancing his academic and technical skills.
