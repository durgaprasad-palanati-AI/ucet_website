Profile

Mr. Y. Naga Raju joined the University College of Engineering and Technology, Mahatma Gandhi University, Nalgonda, in 2014 as Assistant Professor (C) in the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering. He possesses 12 years of teaching experience in engineering education.

He completed his B.Tech and M.Tech from Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Hyderabad (JNTUH). He is currently pursuing his Ph.D. from Osmania University.

He has served in several important administrative roles, including Coordinator of the Examination Branch and Head of the Department. He also played a key role as a subcommittee member in establishing the Electrical Machines Laboratory, Power Electronics Laboratory, and Power Systems Laboratory in the department.

Presently, he is serving as the Head of the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering and is also the Coordinator of the Placement Cell at University College of Engineering and Technology.
