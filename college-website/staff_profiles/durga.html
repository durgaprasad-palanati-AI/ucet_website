Profile

Mr. P. Durga Prasad is serving as Assistant professor (c) in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at University College of Engineering and Technology, Mahatma Gandhi University, Nalgonda since 2014. He brings with him 14 years of teaching experience and a strong foundation in core computer science disciplines.

He has published 2 papers in International Journals and 2 papers in National Journals, participated in 3 National Seminars, attended 2 Workshops and 1 Faculty Development Programme (FDP), and has also organized a National Seminar, demonstrating active engagement in academic and research activities.

He is currently serving as the In-charge Head of the Department of CSE, and is a member of both the Anti-Ragging Committee and the Disciplinary Committee at the institution.
