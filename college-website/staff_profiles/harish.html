Profile

Dr. K. Harish Kumar is serving as Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science & Informatics at Mahatma Gandhi University, Nalgonda since 4th May 2012. He holds an M.Tech and Ph.D in Computer Science & Engineering and brings 15 years of rich teaching experience. He began his career at MGU as an Academic Consultant in the same department from 2009 to 2012.

He has published 10 papers in International Journals, with 4 indexed in SCI/Web of Science and 6 in Scopus, and has presented 2 papers at International Conferences. He has also authored 1 book and contributed 2 book chapters. He holds 1 Indian Patent to his credit and serves as a reviewer for the Springer Journal — Multimedia Tools and Applications. He is currently guiding 1 Ph.D Research Scholar.

He is a Life Member of the Indian Society for Technical Education (ISTE) and the Computer Society of India (CSI), and is an active member of the Alumni Association and various institutional development committees of MGU.
