Profile

Mrs. Allani Kalpana is serving as Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science & Informatics at Mahatma Gandhi University, Nalgonda since 21 December 2023. She completed her M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University (JNTU) Hyderabad, and also holds a B.Ed in Mathematics, bringing a unique dual academic background to her teaching.

She has 15 years of teaching experience in education. Prior to joining Mahatma Gandhi University, she served as an Assistant Professor at REC and as faculty at a Government Junior College, gaining broad teaching experience across both engineering and pre-university levels.

She has published two research papers in the International Journal of Research: "An Analysis of Multi-Grained Block Management to Enhance" and "A Recital Analysis of Secure and Trustable Routing in Wireless Sensor Networks", reflecting her research interest in data management and network security.
