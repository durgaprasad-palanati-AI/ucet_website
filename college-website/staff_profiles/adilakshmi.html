Profile

Mrs. V. Adilakshmi is currently serving as an Assistant Professor (C) in the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at the University College of Engineering and Technology, Mahatma Gandhi University, Nalgonda. She has been associated with the institution since 2 August 2016.

She completed her B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering from JNTU Hyderabad in 2012 and obtained her M.Tech in Electronic Communications from JNTU Hyderabad in 2014.

With 9 years of teaching experience, she has actively contributed to engineering education and student development. She has participated in several Faculty Development Programmes organized by EDUNET to enhance her professional and technical competencies.

She has served as Head of the Department and has actively participated in various institutional committees, including the Admissions Committee and Sports Committee, contributing to both academic administration and student engagement activities.
