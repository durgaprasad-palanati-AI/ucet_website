Profile

K. Lavanya joined the institute in 2023 and has 10 years of teaching experience in the field of Electrical and Electronics Engineering.

Her teaching areas include Electrical Circuits, Electrical Machines, Power Systems, Control Systems, Utilization of Electrical Energy (UEE), and Hybrid Electric Vehicles (HEV), providing strong academic support to undergraduate students.

She has published three research papers in the International Journal of Professional Engineering and actively contributes to academic development through research engagement.

She has also attended one Faculty Development Programme (FDP) conducted at Mahatma Gandhi University, enhancing her professional and academic skills.
