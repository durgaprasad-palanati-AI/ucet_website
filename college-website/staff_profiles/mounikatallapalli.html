Profile

Mrs. Tallapalli Mounika is serving as an Assistant Professor (Contract) in the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at the University College of Engineering and Technology, Mahatma Gandhi University, Nalgonda. She has been associated with the institution since 2018.

She obtained her M.Tech in Power Engineering and Energy Systems from MIST, affiliated with JNTUH, in 2015 and completed her B.Tech in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from VBIT, JNTUH, in 2012.

Her research interests focus on Smart Grids and Electric Vehicle (EV) Batteries, with an emphasis on developing innovative solutions for efficient energy management and sustainable power systems.

She is dedicated to teaching and mentoring students in emerging areas of electrical engineering while actively contributing to research in renewable energy and electric mobility technologies.
