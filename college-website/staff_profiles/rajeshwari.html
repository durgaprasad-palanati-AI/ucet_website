Profile

Mrs. A. Rajeshwari has been serving as Assistant Professor (Contract) in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at University College of Engineering & Technology, Mahatma Gandhi University, Nalgonda since 2 August 2016. She completed her B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from Nagarjuna Institute of Technology & Science, Miryalaguda in 2012, and her M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from the same institution in 2014.

She brings 10 years of teaching experience in the field of Computer Science and Engineering. In addition to her teaching responsibilities, she has served as Head of the Department and Department Coordinator for the Department of CSE, reflecting her capability in both academic and administrative functions.

She is an active member of various institutional committees, including the Admissions Committee and the Cultural Committee. She has also been serving as Warden of Krishnaveni Girls' Hostel from 2021 to date, demonstrating her commitment to student welfare and campus life.
