Profile

Mr. K. Avinash Babu is currently serving as an Assistant Professor (C) in the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at the University College of Engineering and Technology, Mahatma Gandhi University, Nalgonda. He has been associated with the institution since 14 August 2014.

He completed his B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering from JNTU Hyderabad in 2007 and obtained his M.Tech in Embedded Systems from JNTU Hyderabad in 2010. He is currently pursuing his Ph.D. at Biju Patnaik University of Technology (BPUT), Odisha.

With 14 years of teaching experience, he has made significant contributions to engineering education. He has actively participated in workshops, seminars, and Faculty Development Programmes to enhance his academic and technical expertise.

He has served as Head of the Department and has been actively involved in various university committees, including Admissions Committee, Cultural Committee, and Hostel Administration. His contributions extend beyond academics through his role as Hostel Warden and involvement in student welfare activities.
