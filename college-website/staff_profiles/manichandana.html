Profile

Mrs. Dudaka Mani Chandana is working as an Assistant Professor (PT) in the Department of Computer Science & Informatics at Mahatma Gandhi University, Nalgonda, since 28 February 2025.

She obtained her B.Tech (CSE) and M.Tech (CSE) qualifications and completed her M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from Anurag Engineering College, Kodad, Nalgonda District.

She possesses 6 years of teaching experience in higher education and has contributed to the academic growth and development of students through effective teaching and mentoring.

Prior to joining Mahatma Gandhi University, she worked as an Assistant Professor at MIETW. In addition to her academic experience, she has 2 years of industry experience as a Software Test Engineer at Vodafone, Pune, which provides her with valuable practical insights into software development and testing methodologies.
