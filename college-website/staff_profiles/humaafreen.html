Profile

Mrs. Huma Afreen is currently serving as an Assistant Professor (C) in the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at the University College of Engineering and Technology, Mahatma Gandhi University, Nalgonda. She has been associated with the institution since 2 August 2016.

She completed her B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering from JNTU Hyderabad in 2012 and obtained her M.Tech in VLSI System Design from JNTU Hyderabad in 2014.

With 9 years of teaching experience, she has contributed significantly to undergraduate engineering education. She has actively participated in Faculty Development Programmes, including programmes conducted by Edunet, to enhance her teaching and technical expertise.

In addition to her academic responsibilities, she has served as Assistant Coordinator for the Examination Branch, contributing to the effective administration of academic and examination-related activities.
