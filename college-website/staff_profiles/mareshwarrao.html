Profile

G. Mareswara Rao is serving as Assistant Professor (C) in the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at University College of Engineering and Technology, Mahatma Gandhi University, Nalgonda, since 2014.

He received his Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering from JNTU Hyderabad, Telangana, and obtained his Master of Technology (M.Tech) in Digital Electronics and Communication Systems from Vardhaman College of Engineering, affiliated with JNTU Hyderabad.

He has qualified UGC-NET and is currently pursuing his Ph.D. in VLSI at JNTU Hyderabad. He possesses more than 11 years and 6 months of teaching experience in engineering education.

He served as Head of the Department of ECE from 2017 to 2019 and was a member of the subcommittee responsible for establishing laboratories in the Department of ECE. He has taught several undergraduate courses including VLSI, Information Theory, Electromagnetic Fields and Transmission Lines, Signals and Systems, Communication Systems, and Digital System Design.

He has supervised numerous undergraduate student projects in the area of VLSI and has published two research papers in international journals and conference proceedings.
