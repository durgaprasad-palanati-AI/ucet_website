Profile

Mr. Yerukala Saidulu is working as an Assistant Professor (PT) in the Department of Computer Science & Informatics at Mahatma Gandhi University, Nalgonda, since 28 January 2025. He completed his M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University (JNTU).

He has 11 years of teaching experience and has contributed significantly to technical education. Prior to joining Mahatma Gandhi University, he served as an Assistant Professor at the University College of Engineering & Technology.

Currently, he is pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering at SR University, Warangal, further strengthening his academic and research expertise. He has published two research papers indexed in Scopus. In addition, one Scopus journal paper and one SCI-indexed research paper are presently under review.

He has actively trained students under the National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC) programme of Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY), helping learners acquire essential computer skills. He has also trained students in Arithmetic and Reasoning for competitive examinations, enabling them to develop strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
