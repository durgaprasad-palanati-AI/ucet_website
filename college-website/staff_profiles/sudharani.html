Profile

Dr. Ch. Sudha Rani is an accomplished academician with over 17 years of teaching experience in Computer Science and Engineering. She earned her B.Tech in Computer Science and Information Technology in 2003, followed by an M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering in 2008, and was awarded her Ph.D in Computer Science and Engineering in 2025.

She is currently serving as Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Informatics at University College of Engineering and Technology, Mahatma Gandhi University, Nalgonda. She has also held significant administrative responsibilities, including Head of the Department of CS&I and Principal of UCET, MGU.

Dr. Sudha Rani has contributed to the academic community through seven research papers published in National and International Journals, and has presented her research at four National and International Conferences. She is also the author of the book "Advanced Machine Learning Applications using Python Programming", published in 2022.
