Profile

Dr. Kanigiri Bharathi is working as an Assistant Professor (PT) in the Department of Computer Science & Informatics at Mahatma Gandhi University, Nalgonda, since 28 February 2025. She completed her Ph.D. in Computer Science & Engineering from JJTU and possesses strong academic, research, and teaching expertise.

She has 11 years of teaching experience and has previously served as an Assistant Professor at MIETW, NITS, and SCTC Engineering Colleges. Throughout her academic career, she has been actively involved in teaching, student mentoring, and institutional development activities.

Her research contributions include 5 research publications, presentations in 2 National Conferences and 2 International Conferences, and active participation in 30 Faculty Development Programmes (FDPs). One of her SCI-indexed research papers is currently under process.

She has successfully guided 15 Undergraduate and 8 Postgraduate student projects and remains committed to promoting research excellence, innovation, and quality education among students.
