Profile

Prof. Rekha Redamalla is currently serving as Professor of Computer Science and Informatics, Principal at University College of Engineering and Technology, and Dean of the Faculty of Engineering and Technology, Mahatma Gandhi University, Nalgonda. She holds an MCA from Sri Padmavathi Mahila Vishawa Vidhyalayam, Tirupathi; an M.Tech in Computer Science from JNTUH (Campus), Hyderabad; and a Ph.D from the University of Udine, Italy.

She was appointed as Associate Professor at Mahatma Gandhi University in April 2012 and was promoted to Professor in 2022. Prior to joining MGU, she served as Head of the Department of CSE for about 10 years and as Principal at Bhoj Reddy Engineering College for Women, Hyderabad in 2011.

She has published twenty-one research papers in International and National Journals and Conferences, including publications in Springer, Elsevier, Cambridge University Press, and UGC Care listed journals. She is a Life Member of the Indian Society for Technical Education (LMISTE) and has been recognised as an NPTEL Discipline Star for December 2020 by NPTEL-Swayam. She received the B N Sastry Memorial Personal Talent Award for the Year 2006.

One Research Scholar has been awarded a Ph.D in CSE under her guidance, and three more Research Scholars are currently pursuing their doctoral work under her supervision.
