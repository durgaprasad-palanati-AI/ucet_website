Principal's Message

Welcome to University College of Engineering and Technology, Mahatma Gandhi University

“Education is not the learning of facts, but the training of the mind to think.”
— Albert Einstein

It is my pleasure to welcome you to the University College of Engineering and Technology (UCET), Mahatma Gandhi University website. It is a privilege to serve as the Principal of this esteemed institution and to work alongside a dedicated team of faculty members, staff, and students who continuously strive for excellence.

Engineering education has become an integral and indispensable part of modern society. Education becomes truly meaningful when it nurtures natural talent, develops critical thinking, and transforms students into responsible and value-based individuals.

The purpose of education is not merely to impart knowledge but to cultivate the ability to think, reason, innovate, and solve real-world challenges. At UCET, this objective is achieved through an excellent academic environment, state-of-the-art infrastructure, well-equipped laboratories, and highly qualified faculty members committed to student success.

The college has a strong tradition of academic excellence and holistic personality development. Our curriculum and teaching methodologies are designed to provide students with a strong foundation in theoretical concepts while ensuring practical and industry-oriented learning experiences. We actively promote Outcome Based Education (OBE) to meet global academic and professional standards.

Students are provided opportunities to interact with industry experts, researchers, and professionals through workshops, seminars, internships, and training programs. Through initiatives such as TASK and ICT-enabled learning, we focus on enhancing technical, communication, leadership, and entrepreneurial skills to prepare students for a competitive global environment.

UCET also encourages participation in co-curricular and extracurricular activities, helping students discover and develop their talents beyond academics. We believe that these experiences play a vital role in shaping confident, socially responsible, and well-rounded individuals.

I wish every student a rewarding and successful academic journey at UCET. May your time here be filled with learning, innovation, personal growth, and memorable achievements that contribute to the pride and prestige of this institution.

I warmly welcome you all and extend my best wishes for a bright and successful future.

Prof. Rekha Redamalla
M.Tech., Ph.D. (Italy), LMISTE
Principal
University College of Engineering and Technology
Mahatma Gandhi University, Nalgonda
